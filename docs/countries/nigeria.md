# Nigeria

Nigeria is the v0.1 reference country.

- Country code: `NG`
- Currency: NGN (minor-unit exponent 2)
- Time zone: Africa/Lagos
- Implemented payment adapters: local mock, Paystack, Flutterwave
- Planned after payment stability: Termii and Africa's Talking messaging

Capability claims are returned from `@africa-dev/country-data`. Other country profiles exist for currency/localization groundwork, but planned entries are not returned as usable capabilities.
